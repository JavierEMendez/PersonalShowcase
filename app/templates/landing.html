01 · Residential land

MPC Underwriting

A master planned community land model that runs in the browser. Enter tract, cost, and revenue inputs and the engine returns developable acreage after net-outs, lot allocation by front footage, section pacing, takedown revenue with builder earnest money, MUD and WCID reimbursements, and an unlevered IRR on the closing-date convention. Scenarios can be saved, compared, and promoted.

InputsTract, cost, revenue, and lookup tables
ReturnsMonthly pro forma, margins, IRR, per-acre and per-lot metrics
AnalysisNamed scenarios, two-axis sensitivity grid, change history
ExportExcel with live formulas, PDF summary

02 · Income property

Deal-to-Portfolio Copilot

One workflow from offering memo to asset management. Screen extracts assumptions from the OM and rent roll with a source citation for each figure. Underwrite builds the NOI, capital stack, and stress table and exports the model to Excel. Recommend drafts the investment committee memo from the model. Monitor tracks covenants, DSCR, and maturities after close.

UnderwriteNOI build, sources and uses, debt sizing, sensitivities, downside case

Method

How it was built

The code was written with Claude Code against a written standard. The standard, the decision log, and the tests are in the repository.

Reconciled to ExcelEvery model function has a test that reproduces a hand-checked case from the original workbook. A change that breaks reconciliation fails the build.
Decisions on recordProduct and architecture decisions are dated entries in docs/decisions.md: what was chosen, why, and what was rejected.
Evaluated, then trustedThe two steps that use a language model, extraction and memo drafting, ship with eval sets and a written list of failure modes. Model math never runs through a language model.
Synthetic by designDemo deals are invented. Market inputs come from public sources: FRED, Census, appraisal district records, and SEC filings. No client data is used.
